About the Partnership

For years, Salado Village Framer has been Central Texas's trusted source for custom framing, conservation, and curated décor. The Trade Collective extends this craft to the interior design community. From museum-quality framing to shadow boxes, conservation mounting to decorative mirrors — we serve as your dedicated framing partner from concept through installation.

As a design professional, you understand the impact of thoughtful materials and precise execution. Our workshop team brings decades of experience to every project, offering guidance at every stage — from initial concept sketches through final installation. We work at your pace, respecting your vision and timeline.

Sister Business Partnership

As a sister business to Solas Gallery, Trade Collective membership extends across both businesses — giving you access to fine art, custom commissions, large-format printing, and museum-quality framing under one partnership. This dual access strengthens your ability to serve your clients with comprehensive design solutions.

Our Services

Custom Framing

Museum-quality materials, hundreds of moulding profiles, and endless design possibilities. We work with artwork, textiles, prints, and more.

Conservation & Archival

Acid-free mounting, UV glass, and heirloom preservation techniques. Your clients' treasured pieces deserve expert care.

Shadow Boxes & Specialty

Jerseys, memorabilia, dimensional objects, and bespoke solutions. We frame what matters most.

Decorative Mirrors

Custom and curated mirror framing solutions that enhance any interior. From statement pieces to coordinated sets.

Commercial & Volume Framing

Multi-piece projects for offices, hotels, restaurants, and hospitality spaces. Consistency, quality, and timeline reliability.

Design Consultation

On-site visits, material selection, and collaborative project planning. We integrate seamlessly into your design process.
