About the Partnership

Salado Village Framer is a custom frame shop in Salado, Texas. The Trade Collective extends that work to interior designers: framing for art and photographs, shadow boxes, conservation framing and mirrors, with one shop handling each project from first conversation to finished piece.

Cherie Flanagan, a certified picture framer with thirty years at the bench, guides each project, and every piece is framed in our Salado shop. We work at your pace and around your vision and timeline.

Our Services

Custom Framing

A wide range of mouldings and mats for artwork, textiles, prints and photographs.

Conservation & Archival

Archival mats, UV-filtering glazing and reversible mounting for pieces your clients want to keep.

Shadow Boxes & Specialty

Jerseys, medals, textiles and other objects that carry a story.

Decorative Mirrors

Custom frames for statement mirrors and large pieces.

Multi-Piece Projects

Framing several pieces for one project? Tell us the count and sizes and we will plan it together.

Design Consultation

Material selection and project planning in the shop, with you and your client.
